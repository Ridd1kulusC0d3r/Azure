# Detection Lifecycle

## 1. Proposal
Detection opportunity identified from intelligence, threat modeling, incidents or hunting.

## 2. Telemetry validation
Confirm the necessary tables and fields exist in the target environment.

## 3. Query development
Create readable logic with explicit time windows and minimal unnecessary processing.

## 4. Baseline
Measure normal behavior before selecting thresholds.

## 5. Validation
Generate or identify authorized test activity and confirm the expected signal.

## 6. Tuning
Document exclusions and accepted residual noise.

## 7. Deployment
Promote into the target analytics platform with versioned metadata.

## 8. Measurement
Track firing rate, analyst disposition, false positives and coverage value.

## 9. Review
Reassess logic when schemas, products, threats or business processes change.
